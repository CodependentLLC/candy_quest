# Progression & Challenge Steering

## Progression layers

Candy Quest can grow across:

1. level progression
2. world progression
3. character unlocks
4. star mastery
5. score mastery
6. optional challenge objectives

## Avoid grind

For kids, progression should reward successful play and exploration rather than repeated mandatory grinding.

## Stars

Use stars as mastery markers:
- one on main path
- one for exploration
- one for skill/challenge

Avoid hiding all stars behind obscure secrets.

## Optional objectives

Examples:
- collect 80% of candy
- finish with 2+ lives
- finish with 10+ seconds remaining
- defeat all enemies
- find secret candy cache

Objectives should not be required for basic level completion.

## Difficulty bands

### Easy
Large platforms, slow enemies, generous checkpoints.

### Normal
Mechanic combinations and moderate timing.

### Challenge
Optional routes with tighter windows and higher rewards.

Main progression should avoid sudden precision spikes.

## Unlock rules

Characters may unlock through:
- world completion
- star totals
- achievement milestones

Do not make a character mandatory unless all players can reasonably unlock it before the requirement.

## Replayability

Prefer:
- better score
- star completion
- alternate routes
- optional challenge pickups
- time improvement

over randomized unfair hazards.
